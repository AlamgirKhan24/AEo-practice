# Competitor tools and platform analysis

## Research note

Official Google Search Central AI-feature guidance and the Bing Webmaster Tools public landing page were inspected on 22 November 2026; this is a focused public documentation review, not a paid-tool procurement evaluation. This document deliberately avoids claiming market share, coverage counts, or unverified feature releases. Confirm current availability, terms, geography, and plan limits before procurement.

| Tool/platform | Role in an audit | Strength | Limitation / how to use responsibly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search Console | Search performance and indexing signals for verified site owners | Google states AI-feature traffic is included in the overall Web search performance reporting | Not an exhaustive AI citation log; requires verified property and does not guarantee attribution for every AI interaction |
| Bing Webmaster Tools | Bing search performance and site diagnostics | The current public landing page lists SEO/GEO tools, keyword research, Site Scan, SEO/GEO reports, and backlinks | Requires account/site access for data; product coverage/UI can change; not representative of every answer engine |
| Google Rich Results Test | Test supported Google rich-result eligibility for a URL/code | Official validation for supported result types | Eligibility is not a promise of display or AI citation |
| Schema.org Validator | Parse and inspect Schema.org vocabulary usage | General structured-data vocabulary validation | Does not certify Google eligibility or factual accuracy |
| Screaming Frog SEO Spider / Sitebulb | Authorized website crawling and technical sampling | URL-level crawl and internal-link data | Configuration/rendering/sample quality matter; respect authorization and crawl limits |
| Lighthouse / PageSpeed Insights | Lab and field performance diagnostics where data exists | Reproducible performance/accessibility clues | Lab score is not an AEO score; field data may be absent or aggregated |
| Google Trends / Keyword Planner | Topic/trend and query-demand research | Supports question/topic discovery | Directional or modeled data, not exact demand for every niche/query |
| Manual answer-engine prompt log | Sample visibility/citation observations | Auditable query, date, platform and citation record | Labor-intensive and non-exhaustive; outputs fluctuate; no implied API access |

## Competitive differentiation opportunity

Most SEO suites focus on traditional crawl, rank, backlink, or traffic tasks. A useful audit system can differentiate through (1) explicit evidence provenance for every finding, (2) readiness scoring separated from observed AI citations, (3) exact prompt/run logs with market and date, (4) confidence and sample coverage, and (5) owner-ready prioritized recommendations. Do not market an “AI visibility score” as authoritative unless its dataset, sampling, repeatability, and validation are clearly explained.
