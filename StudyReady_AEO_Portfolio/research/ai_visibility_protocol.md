# AI answer visibility protocol

No AI citation results are claimed for the local prototype. Use this protocol after public deployment.

1. Select 15–20 representative questions from the validated research set. Freeze the exact wording and track variants separately.
2. Select answer products available to the project, such as Google AI features where surfaced, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Copilot. Record product and access mode.
3. Run the same prompt set on a consistent schedule and locale. Log date, locale, exact prompt, product, answer availability, cited domains, exact URLs, and whether the site is named or cited.
4. Check whether each citation supports the statement attributed to it. Classify the result as correct citation, incorrect or misleading citation, no citation, or no answer.
5. Report sample size, denominator, and limitations. Results vary by time, location, account, and product; do not infer a ranking factor or causal effect from a small sample.
6. Keep Search Console and Bing Webmaster metrics separate from observed AI answer citations.
