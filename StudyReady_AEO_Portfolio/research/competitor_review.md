**Status:** This is a research shortlist and live-review plan, not a completed page-by-page audit. Outbound search failed during this project run, so URLs and current page details could not be checked live. The comparison angles below are hypotheses based on each organization’s broad public positioning. No claims about rankings, traffic, schema, citations, or current page design are made.

| Organization | Model | What to inspect in a live review | Strategic relevance |

| Harvard Academic Resource Center | University academic support | Verify URL and inspect resources, authorship, and accessibility. | Potential institutional reference; current endpoint needs verification. |

## Page-level comparison rubric

For two or three representative pages per organization, record URL and access date; user intent; page purpose; answer placement; depth and originality; cited sources and authorship; headings and internal links; visible and machine-readable markup; accessibility and mobile presentation; supported search features; and a concrete content gap. Separate observed facts from interpretation. Do not estimate rankings, traffic, or AI citations without a reliable source.
