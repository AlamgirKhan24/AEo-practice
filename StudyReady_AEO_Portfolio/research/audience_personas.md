# Audience personas and validation assumptions

These are working hypotheses based on the agreed illustrative niche, not interview findings. Validate them with learners and educators before treating them as representative.

| Persona | Situation | Job to be done | Friction | Content response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independent student, roughly 13–18 | Revising with mixed confidence and limited time; may use a phone. | Choose one helpful next action and tell if it worked. | Vague advice, unfamiliar terms, uncertainty about which method to use. | Direct answer, concrete steps, worked example, self-check, next guide. |
| Supportive caregiver | Wants to support learning without taking over. | Understand how to help plan and encourage practice. | Advice may not fit the student’s subject, school, or needs. | Shareable guide, clear boundaries, questions to discuss with a teacher. |
| Teacher or tutor | Needs material to assign, adapt, or share. | Find a stable resource with a clear learning focus. | Unclear authorship, unsupported claims, no curriculum alignment. | Source notes, reviewer field, audience scope, accessible page. |

## Interview prompts

- Tell me about the last time you were unsure how to study a topic. What did you do next?
- Which advice or resources helped, and what was missing?
- What words do you use for revision, tests, and study routines?
- How do you decide that you understand a topic?
- Which device, language, and accessibility needs should the site support?
- Which country, curriculum, or exam system must examples reflect?
