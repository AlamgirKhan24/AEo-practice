## Re-entry steps when access is available

1. Open and verify each source URL; record the date and relevant page title.
2. Review two or three representative pages for each competitor and fill the live-review rubric.
3. Collect real learner and educator language, then reconcile the 35 question hypotheses.
4. Inspect current search results for a documented locale and date; capture only observable features.
5. Verify the final public site's URLs and structured data with official validators.
