About this project

Study support with the reasoning in view

StudyReady is a portfolio prototype for a free study-skills library for secondary-school students.

Who it is for

The initial concept serves learners around ages 13–18, plus caregivers and educators. It is English-first and geography-neutral. No curriculum or accreditation is claimed.

Editorial standards

  • Name real authors and qualified reviewers on a public site.
  • Link primary research or established education guidance for learning claims.
  • Explain what is general guidance and what depends on a curriculum or learner.
  • Provide a correction route and dated review record.
  • Keep learning information accessible in page text, not only images or downloads.

Review status

This prototype has not been reviewed by an educator or tested with students. Before publication, assign a content owner, review each claim and link, and provide a contact route for corrections.
