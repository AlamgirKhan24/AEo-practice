# How to choose a power bank for your phone

**Draft buying guide · Product availability and airline rules must be checked from current primary sources before publication.**

## Start with your device and routine

Check your phone's battery capacity, charging connector, supported charging protocols, and the kind of outlet access you usually have. For commuting, size and weight may matter as much as capacity. For multi-device use, count the ports and check whether the unit can provide the required output to more than one device at a time.

## Compare capacity carefully

Power-bank capacity is commonly advertised in mAh, but the energy available to a phone is lower than the label implies because voltage conversion and device charging create losses. A conceptual estimate is:

`estimated charges ≈ power-bank usable energy (Wh) × conversion efficiency ÷ phone battery energy (Wh)`

Use exact manufacturer values and disclose the efficiency assumption if providing a numeric estimate. Real results vary with device temperature, screen use, cable, charging protocol, age and charging conditions. Do not promise a specific number of charges from the mAh label alone.

## Confirm charging compatibility

“USB-C” describes a connector, not a guaranteed charging speed. Verify supported protocols and output wattage for the exact power-bank model, phone and cable. A device may charge at a lower rate when the combination does not support the same protocol.

## Check size, contents and safeguards

Read the exact model's dimensions and weight, what accessories are included, and the manufacturer's instructions. For travel, check current airline and aviation authority requirements for lithium batteries and carry-on limits; rules can vary by operator and can change. Do not rely on an old blog post for a flight decision.

## Compare the offer, not just the headline price

Before purchase, review the exact variant, seller, current total, delivery estimate after entering your location, available payment methods at checkout, warranty provider and item-specific return terms. These facts may differ between listings and can change over time.

## Quick checklist

- Exact model and rated capacity match your need.
- Output protocol and wattage are compatible with your phone and cable.
- Ports and included accessories suit your devices.
- Size/weight fit your daily carry or travel use.
- Seller, warranty, delivery estimate and return eligibility are clear for the selected offer.
- Current travel rules have been checked with the airline/authority if flying.
