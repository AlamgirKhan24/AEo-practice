## Shopper questions

### How many times can this model charge my phone?

We cannot give a reliable charge count until the exact model's usable energy/output and your phone model are known. Battery conversion losses and phone use affect the result. Add an estimate only with a disclosed formula, source data and caveat.

### Does this model support fast charging?

Confirm the exact model's charging protocol and output from manufacturer documentation. A “fast charging” marketing phrase alone is not enough to guarantee compatibility with a particular phone.

### What comes in the box?

List each included item from the current package for this variant. Do not infer a cable or adapter from a product image.

### Can I return it?

Review the live offer and current return policy for this item and order. Eligibility and timing can depend on policy conditions; the checkout/account pages are authoritative for a placed order.
