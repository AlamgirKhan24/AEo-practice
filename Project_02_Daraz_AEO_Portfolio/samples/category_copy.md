## Choose a power bank by device, capacity and charging needs

Compare power banks by the exact capacity, ports, supported output and size that match your device and routine. A larger mAh number does not by itself tell you how many full charges you will get: conversion losses, the power bank's rated output, and your phone's battery capacity affect the result.

### What to compare

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Capacity and units | Compare like-for-like ratings and verify the stated value against the exact model's documentation. |
| Output and charging protocol | Fast charging requires compatible power bank, cable and device; check exact supported standards and wattage. |
| Ports and included cable | Confirm connector type and whether a cable or adapter is included. |
| Size and weight | Higher capacity can mean a larger/heavier unit; check dimensions for daily carry. |
| Seller, warranty and return terms | These can vary by listing. Read the offer-specific information before buying. |
| Delivered price and arrival estimate | Confirm the current total and delivery estimate after selecting your location. |

### Common questions

**How many times will a power bank charge my phone?** Use the power bank's usable energy rather than assuming its printed mAh transfers in full. A rough estimate is: usable power-bank Wh × conversion efficiency ÷ phone battery Wh. Efficiency and device use vary, so treat the result as an estimate, not a guarantee.

**Will a USB-C power bank fast-charge my phone?** Not necessarily. Check the exact output protocol, wattage, cable and phone compatibility in manufacturer documentation.

**Can I return a power bank if it does not suit me?** Eligibility depends on the current policy and the specific item/order. Review the live return terms shown for the offer and at checkout.
