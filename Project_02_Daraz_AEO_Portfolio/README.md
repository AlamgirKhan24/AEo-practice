# Project 02 — Daraz Pakistan E-commerce AEO Case Study

**Independent public-information case study · Research snapshot: 22 November 2026**

This portfolio project evaluates public Daraz Pakistan pages and develops practical Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), product content, technical SEO, and measurement recommendations. It is an external case study. No access to Daraz systems was available or used, and no site changes were made.

## Evidence and limitations

Daraz homepage content was inspected in a browser on the research snapshot date. Homepage-visible claims and links are recorded in the observation log. One product listing linked from the homepage is used as a sample candidate; its live detail page was not independently verified during this review. Thus product details beyond the visible listing title are explicitly marked unknown and must be checked against the seller's current listing before publication. Competitor market share, traffic, search volume, rankings, and AI citations were not measured. The research tool's search endpoint was unavailable; this is not a substitute for a full crawl, analytics, Search Console, Merchant Center, or seller-side audit.

## How to use

Treat the copy and JSON-LD as templates for an authorized seller-owned storefront. Before publishing, confirm product facts, stock, final PKR price, shipping/returns terms, review counts, seller identity, images, and the canonical product URL. Mark up only information visible to shoppers and keep it synchronized with the page.

## Success measurement

Establish a baseline first. Track eligible product/category impressions and clicks, non-brand question-query visibility, organic revenue/conversion where consent and analytics allow, product rich-result validity, index coverage, and sample answer-engine citations with date/query/screenshot evidence. Report observed results separately from estimates and recommendations.
