# Public observation log

**Observed:** 22 November 2026, browser UI, public Daraz Pakistan homepage (`https://www.daraz.pk/`). This is a single-page snapshot, not a representative crawl.

## Directly observed on the homepage

- The page title presented was “Online Shopping in Pakistan: Fashion, Electronics & Groceries - Daraz.pk”.
- The header exposed a search field, login/sign-up links, cart link, language-switch text, and “Help & Support”.
- Homepage modules included Flash Sale, Categories, Just For You, and a long footer with Customer Care, Daraz, payment-method, and category/brand links.
- Customer Care links included Help Center, How to Buy, Returns & Refunds, Contact Us, Purchase Protection, and pickup points.
- Payment-method logos/text were visible for COD, Visa, Mastercard, Easypay, Daraz Wallet, JazzCash, UnionPay, HBL, and installments. Presence on a homepage does not establish availability for every order or location.
- Homepage editorial copy stated a 7-day return/exchange policy and described card, cash-on-delivery, EMI, wallet, Easypaisa, and JazzCash options. These are Daraz's own claims as rendered in the snapshot; check the linked live terms and order-specific eligibility before repeating them.
- Product cards contained long, keyword-stuffed titles. An example visible card was a Xiaomi Redmi 20000mAh portable power bank title at Rs. 948 with a displayed crossed-out price/discount and 227 ratings. These are transient listing values, personalized/module-dependent, and not endorsed as accurate product attributes. Do not copy the price or rating into evergreen copy/schema.
- Footer content contained internal links to mobile-phone brands, categories, and legacy model/tag URLs. This creates a potential opportunity to review relevance, freshness, and crawl efficiency; an actual crawl is needed to determine indexation or duplication.
